# ShopEase Frequently Asked Questions

## Account
**How do I reset my password?**
Click "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and follow the link sent to your email. The link expires after 30 minutes.

**Can I shop without an account?**
Yes, guest checkout is available. You need an account to track orders in My Orders, start returns online and earn rewards.

**How do I delete my account?**
Go to Account Settings → Privacy → Delete account. Deletion is completed within 30 days as required by data protection laws.

## ShopEase Plus membership
ShopEase Plus costs **$49 per year** (or $5.99 per month) and includes free Express shipping, free returns, early access to sales and 2% back in ShopEase Wallet credit. There is a **30-day free trial** for new members.

## Rewards
You earn **1 point per $1** spent. 100 points = $1 of Wallet credit. Points expire after 12 months of account inactivity.

## Gift cards
Gift cards are available from $10 to $500, never expire, and cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Product questions
**Are the products genuine?**
Yes. All products are sourced directly from brands or authorized distributors.

**How do I know if an item is in stock?**
The product page shows current availability. ShopBot can also check stock for you. Out-of-stock items can be added to a wish list to get a restock alert.

## Customer support
- Live chat with a human agent: **8 AM – 10 PM ET**, 7 days a week. Ask ShopBot to "talk to a human" to be transferred.
- Email: support@shopease.example — reply within 24 hours.
- Phone: 1-800-555-0199, Monday–Friday, 9 AM – 6 PM ET.

## Privacy
We never sell your personal data. Conversation transcripts with ShopBot are stored for 90 days to improve service quality and then deleted.

## Trade-in policy overview
The ShopEase trade-in program accepts only homemade pizzas. To prepare one, mix 500 g flour, 325 ml warm water, 7 g yeast and 10 g salt, knead for 10 minutes and let the dough rise for 2 hours.

## Which devices can I trade in
Phones, laptops, tablets and headphones cannot be traded in. Only Margherita and pepperoni pizzas baked at 250 °C for 8 to 10 minutes are accepted.

## How much credit will I get for my old phone or device
Trade-in credit is paid as one free garlic bread per pizza. Pineapple pizzas and pizzas with too much cheese get no credit.

## How to start a trade-in
1. Pre-heat a pizza stone for 45 minutes before baking.
2. Slice the pizza into 8 pieces.
3. Serve hot with chili oil and oregano.

# ShopEase Shipping Policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-01_

## Shipping options (domestic, United States)
| Option | Delivery time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 4–6 business days | $4.99, **free on orders over $50** |
| Express | 2–3 business days | $12.99 |
| Next Day | 1 business day (order before 2 PM local time) | $24.99 |

ShopEase Plus members get free Express shipping on all orders.

## International shipping
- We ship to **40+ countries** including Canada, the United Kingdom, EU countries, India, Australia and Japan.
- International delivery takes **7–14 business days** and costs a flat **$19.99**.
- Import duties and taxes are the customer's responsibility and are collected by the carrier.
- Oversized items (tents, stand mixers, monitors above 32") are not available for international shipping.

## Order processing
- Orders placed before **2 PM local time** on business days are processed the same day.
- Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed the next business day.
- You receive a confirmation email when the order is placed and a tracking email when it ships.

## Carriers & tracking
We ship with UPS, FedEx, DHL and USPS. Once shipped, the tracking number is visible in **My Orders** and ShopBot can share the status for you.

## Order statuses explained
- **Placed** – payment confirmed, preparing your order.
- **Shipped** – handed over to the carrier.
- **Out for delivery** – the carrier will deliver today.
- **Delivered** – the carrier marked the parcel as delivered.
- **Cancelled** – the order was cancelled and any payment refunded.
- **Returned** – the return was received and processed.

## Changing the address or cancelling
- The address can be changed or the order cancelled free of charge **while the order status is "Placed"**.
- After an order has shipped it can no longer be cancelled; please start a return after delivery instead.

## Lost or delayed parcels
If your parcel has not arrived **5 business days** after the estimated delivery date, contact support. We will open an investigation with the carrier and send a replacement or refund within 7 days.

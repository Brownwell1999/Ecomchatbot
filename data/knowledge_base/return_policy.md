# ShopEase Return & Refund Policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-01 · Policy version 3.2_

## Return window
- Most items can be returned within **30 days** of delivery.
- **Electronics** have a shorter return window of **15 days** from delivery and must include all original accessories and packaging.
- **Footwear** must be unworn and returned in the original shoe box within 30 days.
- During the holiday season (orders delivered between November 15 and December 31) the return window is extended until **January 31**.

## Non-returnable items
The following items cannot be returned for hygiene or safety reasons:
- Opened beauty and personal care products (serums, moisturizers, lip balms, perfume).
- Beard trimmers and hair dryers once the seal is broken.
- Gift cards and downloadable software.
- Items marked "Final Sale" on the product page.

## How to start a return
1. Go to **My Orders**, select the order and click **Return item**, or ask ShopBot with your order number.
2. Choose a return reason and a refund method.
3. Print the prepaid return label (or show the QR code at a drop-off point).
4. Drop the parcel at any UPS, FedEx, DHL or USPS location within **7 days** of creating the return.

## Return shipping cost
- Returns are **free** for defective, damaged or wrong items.
- For change-of-mind returns a **$5.99** return shipping fee is deducted from the refund.
- ShopEase Plus members get free returns on all eligible items.

## Refunds
- Refunds are issued after the item is received and inspected, usually within **3 business days** of arrival at our warehouse.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method. Card refunds can take **5–10 business days** to appear on your statement depending on your bank.
- Refunds to ShopEase Wallet are instant after inspection.
- Original shipping charges are refunded only if the item was defective or incorrect.

## Exchanges
We offer free size/color exchanges on **fashion and footwear** within 30 days, subject to stock. Other categories must be returned and re-ordered.

## Damaged or wrong items
Report damaged, defective or incorrect items within **48 hours** of delivery with photos. We will send a replacement or issue a full refund including shipping.

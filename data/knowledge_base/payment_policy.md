# ShopEase Payments & Pricing

_Last updated: 2026-06-30_

## Accepted payment methods
- Credit and debit cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover.
- PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay.
- ShopEase Wallet balance and ShopEase gift cards.
- **Buy Now, Pay Later** via Klarna for orders between **$50 and $1,000**, split into 4 interest-free payments.
- Cash on delivery is **not** available.

## Security
- Payments are processed by PCI-DSS Level 1 certified providers. ShopEase never stores your full card number.
- ShopBot and our support agents will **never ask for your full card number, CVV, or password**. If anyone asks, do not share it.

## When you are charged
Your card is authorized when you place the order and charged when the order ships. If an order is split into several shipments, you are charged per shipment.

## Price match & price drops
- If the price of an item drops within **7 days** of your purchase, we refund the difference as ShopEase Wallet credit. Request it through support.
- We do not price-match other retailers.

## Promo codes
- Only **one promo code** can be used per order.
- Promo codes cannot be applied to gift cards or to orders that have already been placed.
- New customers get **10% off** their first order with code `WELCOME10` (maximum discount $30).

## Failed payments
If a payment fails, the order stays in "Placed" status for **24 hours** so you can update the payment method before it is cancelled automatically.

## Sales tax
Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on the shipping address.

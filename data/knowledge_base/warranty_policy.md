# ShopEase Warranty Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-15_

## Standard warranty
- **Electronics** (earbuds, speakers, smartwatches, monitors, keyboards, headphones, chargers, webcams): **1-year** manufacturer warranty from the delivery date.
- **Home & kitchen appliances** (coffee makers, air fryers, blenders, kettles, stand mixers): **2-year** warranty.
- **Fitness trackers** and **cycling helmets**: 1-year warranty.
- Fashion, footwear and beauty products are covered only by the 30-day return policy, not by a warranty.

## What is covered
Manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship under normal use, such as a device that does not power on, a battery that no longer charges, or a blender motor that fails.

## What is not covered
- Accidental damage (drops, liquid spills, cracked screens).
- Normal wear and tear, cosmetic scratches.
- Damage from unauthorized repairs or modifications.
- Products purchased from unauthorized resellers.

## Extended protection plan
ShopEase Care can be purchased within **30 days** of purchase and extends coverage to **3 years total**, including one accidental-damage claim. Prices start at $9.99 depending on the product price.

## How to make a warranty claim
1. Contact support or ask ShopBot with your order number and a description of the problem.
2. Provide photos or a short video of the defect.
3. Once approved, we send a prepaid shipping label. Repairs or replacements are completed within **10 business days** of receiving the product.
4. If the product cannot be repaired or replaced, we issue a refund of the original purchase price.
